# RajTune 1.4.16

## Highlights

- Add the external-downloader handoff to the player menu while keeping the built-in offline download option.
- Remove Sadie Sink, Sabrina Carpenter, Sabrina Carpenter 2, Millie Bobby Brown, and Billie Eilish from the selectable icon list and settings search; existing saved icon aliases remain compatible.

- Add custom-work UPI options: ₹50 for icon changes, ₹100 for UI themes or small changes, ₹150 for new features, and ₹300 for advanced features or customization.
- Add the developer’s WhatsApp QR link; after paying, customers can message the request and UPI transaction reference. Payment and WhatsApp handoff are manual; the app does not automatically verify payments or unlock paid features.
