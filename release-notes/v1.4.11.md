# Himangshu Music 1.4.11

## Highlights

- Disable backdrop-glass blur in the quick Settings sheet opened from the Settings button, including its Account, Preferences, and App groups.
- Add regression coverage for the Settings backdrop-glass opt-out.

## Notes

This release candidate passed the signed Android CI build. The Settings crash has not yet been reproduced on the affected phone, so the change is a targeted mitigation rather than a confirmed root-cause fix.
