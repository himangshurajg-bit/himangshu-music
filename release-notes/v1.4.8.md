# Himangshu Music 1.4.8

## Highlights

- Add a custom home-screen shortcut: enter a name, choose and preview an image, then request that Android pin the shortcut. The custom name and image apply to that shortcut only; they do not change the installed app's app-drawer name, package identity, or regular app icon. Preset launcher-icon choices remain separate.
- Improve Spotify track matching with anonymous searches, alternate query fallbacks, and title/artist/duration similarity scoring.
- Make the player podcast-aware: hide song lyrics and canvas controls for podcast episodes, with related queue changes.
- Refine player, queue, artwork, navigation, and playback behavior.
- Expand searchable settings and refresh extension-setting labels and help text.

## Custom shortcut limits

Names accept up to 40 characters, while Android's short shortcut label is limited to 25; some launchers may display a shortened label. The selected image is center-cropped to a square and resized to 512 × 512. Pinning depends on launcher support and may require confirmation, and each launcher controls the final shortcut appearance. The custom shortcut does not rename the app in the app drawer or change its regular app icon.

## Latest Echo Music updates

- Improve lossless add-on reliability with adaptive quality selection and playback-order preloading.
- Retry transient host errors (429/503), improve YouTube fallback and seekable FLAC playback, and fix queue and cover handling.
- Keep artwork sharp when a max-resolution thumbnail is unavailable, and prevent automix picks from jumping ahead of radio tracks.
- Show one search-results list only while typing, report lossless quality accurately, and count stalls only after playback starts.
- Avoid crashes when opening external URLs.
