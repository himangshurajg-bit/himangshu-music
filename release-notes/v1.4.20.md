# RajTune 1.4.20

## Highlights

- Add beat/onset-reactive stereo motion and low-, mid-, and high-frequency band positioning to 8D audio.
- Add motion modes and controls for stereo width, bass, ambience, echo and intensity, with a direct PCM bypass when 8D is off.
- Frequency bands still contain overlapping mixed-song content; vocals and instruments are not isolated or separately routed. Beat tracking is a lightweight onset heuristic, not a guaranteed beat grid.
- 8D is stereo panning, not binaural or true 360-degree localization. Physical phone/headphone and USB bit-perfect/offload routing tests have not been completed.
