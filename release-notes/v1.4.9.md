# Himangshu Music 1.4.9

## Highlights

- Add four selectable launcher icons: Neon Headphones, Anime Music, Itachi Crimson, and Itachi Moon.
- Add ten study-focus listening modes and a dim, always-on in-app player screen.
- Add live album-art ambient lighting and liquid-glass styling for supported app surfaces, with reduced-motion behavior.
- Add real-time stereo spatial-audio controls, including adjustable motion and sound effects.

## Notes

The always-on player is an in-app dimmed screen, not Android's system-level always-on display. Spatial audio uses stereo panning rather than HRTF/binaural rendering, and has not been verified on physical phones, headphones, USB DACs, or Bluetooth devices.
